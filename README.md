Muahal is an AI-powered health insurance assistant designed to help patients quickly understand their insurance eligibility and healthcare coverage. The platform allows users to ask whether a medical service, such as an MRI, physiotherapy, blood test, dental care, or specialist visit, is covered by their insurance plan. Muahal can also explain important details such as policy status, copayment, prior authorization requirements, and network restrictions. The system uses structured data, validation, secure tools, and guardrails to provide clear and reliable responses while protecting sensitive information such as policy numbers, national IDs, and phone numbers.
